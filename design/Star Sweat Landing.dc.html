The science, stated plainly

What molecular hydrogen is, and what is actually known about it

Hydrogen gas, H₂, is two hydrogen atoms bound together. It is the smallest and lightest molecule in existence. Dissolve it into water under pressure and you get hydrogen water. The water itself does not change. What changes is that a measurable amount of hydrogen gas is now in it.

Hydrogen is volatile and leaves quickly. Plastic will not hold it. That is why this comes in an aluminum can, sealed, and why we print a tested concentration on the page instead of a marketing number.

Read the literature yourself. Start with PubMed on molecular hydrogen water.

What is known

Hydrogen can be dissolved in water and measured. Concentration is testable, it degrades over time, and packaging determines whether any of it reaches you. Ours is independently tested at a minimum of 2.2 ppm.

What is being studied

Molecular hydrogen has been the subject of a few hundred published papers, most of them small and early. Researchers are still working out what it does in people, at what dose, and over what timeframe.

What we will not say

We are not going to tell you this fixes anything. When our own results are in and reviewed, they go on this page with the methodology attached. Until then you get composition, a test number, and plain description.
