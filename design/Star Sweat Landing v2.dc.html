What molecular hydrogen actually is+

H₂ is two hydrogen atoms bound together — the smallest, lightest molecule in existence. Dissolve it into water under pressure and you have hydrogen water. The water doesn't change; what changes is that a measurable amount of hydrogen gas is now in it. It's volatile and escapes quickly, which is why packaging decides whether any of it reaches you.

What the research does and doesn't show+

Molecular hydrogen has been the subject of a few hundred published papers, most of them small and early. Researchers are still establishing what it does in people, at what dose, over what timeframe. We're not going to tell you it fixes anything. When our own results are in and reviewed, they go on this page with the methodology attached. Read it yourself: PubMed on molecular hydrogen water.

Gold and platinum, and why they're here+

Both are present as trace colloidal elements — suspended particles, not dissolved salts. They're part of the Elemental Water formulation. Specific concentrations and the sourcing detail drop in here once we're cleared to publish them.

Questions, answered

What is Elemental Water?+

A functional beverage: purified water as the base, with molecular hydrogen at 2.2 ppm minimum, magnesium, and trace colloidal gold and platinum. Five things, in a sealed 12 oz can.

How is it different from other hydrogen water?+

Most hydrogen water is a tablet you drop in a glass, a machine on a counter, or a pouch that's lost its hydrogen by the time you open it. Ours is sealed in aluminum, tested per run, and publishes the concentration. It also adds magnesium and trace gold and platinum, which plain hydrogen water doesn't.

How much hydrogen is in each can?+

A minimum of 2.2 ppm dissolved H₂, independently tested. If a run tests under that, it doesn't ship.

Does it taste different?+

Barely. Hydrogen has no taste. Cold, it drinks like clean water with a light crispness from the dissolved gas. Magnesium is the only thing you might notice, faintly.

How should I drink and store it?+

One can a day, cold. Store sealed cans cool and out of direct sun. Once open, finish within about 30 minutes — hydrogen leaves an open can the way carbonation does.
